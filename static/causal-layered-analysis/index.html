Causal Layered Analysis

Examine issues through four progressively deeper layers to create more holistic understanding and genuinely alternative futures

1

Litany

What are the observable trends, events, and data?

2

Social / Systemic Causes

What social, economic, technological, or political structures drive these?

3

Discourse / Worldview

What deeper worldviews, assumptions, or paradigms support these structures?

4

Myth / Metaphor

What deep stories, metaphors, or archetypes underlie these worldviews?

↻

Alternative Future

Work from the deepest layer back up: propose a new myth/metaphor, then envision the new surface reality it would create.
